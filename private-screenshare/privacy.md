_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Private Screenshare for Focusmate does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data.

## What the extension does with your screen

When you start a screen share on focusmate.com, the extension processes the video of your screen inside your own browser. It blurs everything below a strip at the top and passes only that blurred video to the Focusmate page. No frame of your screen, blurred or not, is sent to the developer or to any server the developer runs. The blurred video goes only where Focusmate would have sent your screen share anyway.

## What is stored

The extension saves three settings: whether blurring is on, the height of the sharp strip and the blur strength. They are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync` API. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Google syncs them between your own browsers. They contain nothing about you or your screen.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves the three settings above.
- **Access to focusmate.com and daily.co**: Focusmate's video call runs in a frame hosted on daily.co. The extension needs to run there to blur the screen share. It only acts on daily.co frames that are embedded inside focusmate.com. Daily calls on other sites are left alone.

## Analytics and third parties

There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
